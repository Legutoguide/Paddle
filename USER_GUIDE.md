# Prime Paddle (Sponsor QR Manager) — User Guide

A simple guide for everyday use. No technical knowledge needed.

## Getting Started

**STEP 1** — Open the application (double-click the desktop icon or find it
in the Start Menu).

**STEP 2** — The first time you open it, you'll be asked for your business
name and currency. Enter them and click **Get Started**. This only happens
once.

## Setting Up Your First Coupon Campaign

**STEP 3** — Click **Sponsors** in the left menu, then **New Sponsor**.
Type the sponsor's name (e.g. "Adam") and click **Create Sponsor**.

**STEP 4** — Click **Campaigns**, then **New Campaign**.

**STEP 5** — Choose the sponsor you just created, and give the campaign a
name (e.g. "Summer Promotion").

**STEP 6** — Enter the service (e.g. "1 Hour") and the **Original Price**
(e.g. 100).

**STEP 7** — Enter the **Discount %** (e.g. 50). The final price updates
automatically as you type — you'll see exactly how much the customer saves.

**STEP 8** — Enter how many coupons you need (e.g. 10).

**STEP 9** — Click **Continue**, review the summary, then click
**Generate 10 Codes**.

That's it — 10 unique coupon codes, each with its own QR code, now exist.

## Printing Your Coupons

**STEP 10** — Go to **QR Codes** in the left menu. Tick the checkboxes next
to the codes you want (or tick the box in the header to select all), then
click **Print**. A ready-to-print PDF is created with a QR code, the human-
readable code, the discount, and the price on each coupon.

## Using a Coupon (For Employees)

**STEP 11** — When a customer arrives with a QR code, click
**Scan / Validate** in the left menu — this is the main screen you'll use
every day.

**STEP 12** — Point the camera at the customer's QR code. If you don't have
a camera, or scanning doesn't work, type the code in the box below and
click **Validate**.

**STEP 13** — The screen shows the sponsor, the discount and the final
price. Check that this matches what the customer expects.

**STEP 14** — Click **Confirm Use**. The coupon is now marked as used and
can never be used again — even if someone tries the exact same code twice.

**STEP 15** — The screen automatically resets so you're ready for the next
customer.

## Taking Reservations (Prime Paddle)

**First, set up your business (Admin/CEO, once):** open **Booking Setup** and
enter your opening hours per day (untick *Open* for a closed day), the number
of players per time slot (*capacity*), your time-of-day *periods* (Morning,
Sunset, …) and your *pricing rules*. Until at least one pricing rule matches,
bookings are blocked — the app never guesses a price.

**Walk-in (customer is here now):** *Reservations → Walk-in*. Enter the phone
number (returning customers appear as one-tap suggestions), name and number
of players. The app offers **Available now** or the **Next available** time.
Optionally type a coupon code. Press **Confirm & start**, then **Mark as paid**.

**Customer says "No, tomorrow"?** Press **Book Another Date** — everything you
typed is kept; just pick the date and time.

**Advance reservation:** *Reservations → New Reservation*. Pick a date, a
period and a time. Slots show *Available*, *Almost full*, *Full*, *Closed*,
*In progress* or *Completed*; past slots can never be booked.

**Coupons and payment:** a coupon added to a booking becomes **Reserved** for
that booking — nobody else can use it, and scanning it at *Scan / Validate*
only checks it (it never uses it up). When you press **Mark as paid**, the
payment and the coupon are completed together automatically. If the booking
is cancelled or the customer doesn't show up, the coupon becomes available
again and the slot is freed.

**Calendar:** *Calendar* shows the Day, Week or Month view with real
availability. **Payments** lists what is still unpaid. **Reservation Reports**
shows totals, busiest times/days, sponsor coupon results and can export CSV.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **"INVALID CODE"** — the code doesn't exist. Double-check it was typed or
  scanned correctly.
- **"CODE ALREADY USED"** — this coupon was already redeemed. The screen
  shows exactly when.
- **"CODE EXPIRED"** — the coupon's valid period has ended.
- **"COUPON RESERVED"** — the coupon is already held by a booking and can't
  be used for another one.
- **"CODE REVOKED"** — an administrator canceled this coupon (e.g. it was
  reported lost).

In every one of these cases, nothing is charged and nothing changes — the
customer simply cannot use that code.

## Cancelling a Coupon (Revoke)

If a customer loses a coupon or a mistake was made, go to **QR Codes**, find
the code, and click the revoke icon. Once revoked, that code can never be
used, even if someone finds it later.

## Backing Up Your Data

Your data is important — go to **Settings** → **Backup Database Now**
regularly (for example, once a week) and keep the backup file somewhere
safe (a USB drive, another computer, cloud storage). If your computer ever
has a problem, you can restore everything from that backup file using
**Restore from Backup…** in the same screen.

## Everything Works Without Internet

You can use every single feature of this application — creating sponsors,
generating codes, printing, scanning, and everything else — with your
computer completely disconnected from the internet.
